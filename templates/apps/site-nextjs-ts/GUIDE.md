## Where things are

- `src/site.ts`: the site's name, description, address and navigation
- `src/app/layout.tsx`: the page frame (header, navigation, footer, metadata)
- `src/app/page.tsx`: the home page; `src/app/about/page.tsx`: a second page
- `src/app/sitemap.ts`, `src/app/robots.ts`: for search engines
- `src/app/globals.css`: colors and spacing, light and dark

## Extend it

Copy `src/app/about` to `src/app/<new-page>`, change the text and add the page to `nav` in `src/site.ts`. Set
`NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the real address before deploying so the sitemap and links are right.
